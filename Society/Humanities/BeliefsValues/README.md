# Beliefs and values

Canonical code: `R:U:BV`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).

Worldviews, convictions and principles through which people and communities interpret experience and guide conduct.

Parent: [R:U](../README.md).

Applies to religious and secular beliefs, ethical commitments and shared ideals. Classifying a concept does not assert any person’s adherence or infer sensitive beliefs. Scientific claims belong to their scientific subject; assessments of a person belong to Endorsement.

This is an organizing classification, not an answer field or a claim about a subject.
It defines no answer type, units, choices or value constraints. Classification
does not establish ownership, affiliation, eligibility or verified truth.

## Subclassifications

- [Beliefs (`R:U:BV:B`)](Beliefs/README.md): religious or secular convictions and worldviews; for example a spiritual tradition.
- [Values (`R:U:BV:V`)](Values/README.md): ethical principles and priorities; for example fairness or compassion.

Neither classification has answer fields, types, units or value constraints.
No person's adherence is asserted by these definitions.
