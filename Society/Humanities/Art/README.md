# Art

Canonical code: `R:U:A`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).

Creative expression, artistic works, practices and interpretation.

Parent: [R:U](../README.md).

Applies to visual art, music, literature, theatre, dance and other artistic expression. An artist’s identity belongs to Identity, artistic occupations to Business / Professions, and a purchased cultural service to Society / Services. These examples are not separate canonical subclasses.

This is an organizing classification, not an answer field or a claim about a subject.
It defines no answer type, units, choices or value constraints. Classification
does not establish ownership, affiliation, eligibility or verified truth.

No finer subclasses or answer fields are currently defined.
