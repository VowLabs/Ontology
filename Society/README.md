# Society

Canonical code: `R`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).

Society covers collective life, institutions, governance, law, civic participation,
communities, human culture and interpretation, and services people use. It differs from Identity,
which describes particular people and organizations. Use this branch for shared
social structures and cultural practices, rather than individual identity facts.

Parent: [VowLabs ontology](../README.md).

## Subclassifications

- [Services (`R:SV`)](Services/README.md): Services people consume, organized by
  purpose across commercial, public and nonprofit provision. Categories and
  boundaries are documented in the Services guide; named providers remain data.

- [Humanities (`R:U`)](Humanities/README.md): Human culture, expression, language,
  history, philosophy and interpretation. It applies to the meanings, ideas,
  works and traditions through which people understand human experience.
  Literature and philosophy are scope examples, not separately published codes.
  Humanities currently has no finer classifications or answer fields.

Institutions, governance, law and civic participation remain scope examples;
no separate child classifications or answer fields are published for them yet.

- [Endorsement (`R:EN`)](Endorsement/README.md): An authored free-text assessment
  of an individual or organization with a required one-to-three-star rating.
