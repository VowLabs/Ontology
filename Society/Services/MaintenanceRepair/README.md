# Maintenance and repair

Canonical code: `R:SV:RP`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Work that preserves, restores or improves the usability of buildings and personal goods. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include plumbing, electrical repairs, appliance servicing, vehicle repairs and home renovation.

Ongoing utility supply belongs to Utilities. Buying a replacement product is a goods transaction rather than a repair service.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
