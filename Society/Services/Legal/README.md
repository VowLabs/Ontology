# Legal services

Canonical code: `R:SV:LG`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Advice, representation and assistance concerning rights and legal obligations. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include legal advice, representation, mediation and notarization.

Official public decisions and document issuance belong to Public administration. A law or legal status is not itself a service.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
