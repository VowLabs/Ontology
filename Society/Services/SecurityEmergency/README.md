# Security and emergency assistance

Canonical code: `R:SV:SE`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Protection of people and property and immediate assistance in dangerous situations. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include alarm monitoring, guarding, fire response, rescue and roadside assistance.

Clinical emergency treatment belongs to Healthcare; insurance against loss belongs to Insurance services.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
