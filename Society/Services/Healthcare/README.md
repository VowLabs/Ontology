# Healthcare

Canonical code: `R:SV:HC`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Prevention, assessment, treatment, rehabilitation and health support delivered to people. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include medical consultations, dentistry, therapy and rehabilitation.

Health describes health facts and needs; this category describes the service supplied. Personal grooming belongs to Personal care.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
