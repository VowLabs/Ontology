# Social care and support

Canonical code: `R:SV:SC`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Practical and social assistance supporting daily life, inclusion and wellbeing. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include childcare, elder care, disability support and social assistance casework.

Clinical treatment belongs to Healthcare; routine household cleaning belongs to Household services. Eligibility or a benefit payment is not itself a service.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
