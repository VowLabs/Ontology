# Personal care

Canonical code: `R:SV:PC`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Non-clinical care of personal appearance, hygiene and everyday wellbeing. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include hairdressing, barbering, beauty treatments and non-clinical grooming.

Clinical treatment belongs to Healthcare. Fitness instruction belongs to Recreation and culture.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
