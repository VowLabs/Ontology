# Retail and access services

Canonical code: `R:SV:RT`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Services that help people obtain, exchange or temporarily use consumer goods. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include retail assistance, marketplace intermediation and equipment rental.

The purchased or rented goods remain distinct from the service. Residential accommodation and vehicle hire for travel use their specific categories.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
