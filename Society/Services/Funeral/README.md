# Funeral and bereavement services

Canonical code: `R:SV:FU`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Practical arrangements associated with death, remembrance and disposition of remains. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include funeral arrangements, burial, cremation and memorial services.

Clinical end-of-life care belongs to Healthcare; legal estate administration belongs to Legal services.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
