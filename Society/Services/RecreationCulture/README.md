# Recreation and culture

Canonical code: `R:SV:RC`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Access to or participation in leisure, sport, entertainment and cultural experiences. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include performances, museums, sports facilities, fitness classes and guided leisure activities.

A work of art or a cultural subject is distinct from the service providing access or participation. Formal education belongs to Education.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
