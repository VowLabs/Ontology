# Housing services

Canonical code: `R:SV:HO`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Services that provide access to, manage or facilitate long-term residential accommodation. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include residential letting, property management and housing placement.

A dwelling is a physical asset, not a service. Short-term guest accommodation belongs to Hospitality and travel; repairs belong to Maintenance and repair.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
