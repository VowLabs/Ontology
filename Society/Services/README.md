# Services

Canonical code: `R:SV`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society](../README.md).

Services are activities, assistance or access supplied for people to use or
benefit from. This branch includes paid, public, nonprofit and unpaid provision,
delivered in person or remotely, to individuals or households. It classifies what
a service does, rather than its provider, delivery channel or funding model.

The categories below provide broad initial coverage, not a claim to enumerate
every service. Use the most specific applicable purpose; a bundled offering may
involve several categories. New classifications need their own documented scope.

A physical good, personal condition, account, named provider or software process
is not itself a service classification here. Health, Finance and Business retain
their own facts, products and operational concepts. `S:T:SV` remains the definition
for online account/contact providers; `data/services` remains that provider list.
Named providers belong in reference datasets, not as children of this branch.

## Boundary with Business / Services

[Business / Services (`B:SV`)](../../Business/Services/README.md) contains offerings
aimed at businesses and organizational customers. Society / Services contains
those aimed at individuals and households. The same provider and broad activity
may serve both markets: personal banking belongs here; business banking belongs
under Business. Classify the offering's intended customer, not whether the
provider is a business or whether the service is paid. An incidental reimbursement
by an employer does not change a consumer offering's intended market.

## Subclassifications

- [Healthcare (`R:SV:HC`)](Healthcare/README.md): Prevention, assessment, treatment, rehabilitation and health support delivered to people. Examples: medical consultations, dentistry, therapy and rehabilitation.
- [Education (`R:SV:ED`)](Education/README.md): Teaching, training and guided learning provided to people. Examples: schooling, tutoring, vocational training and adult learning.
- [Housing services (`R:SV:HO`)](Housing/README.md): Services that provide access to, manage or facilitate long-term residential accommodation. Examples: residential letting, property management and housing placement.
- [Utilities (`R:SV:UT`)](Utilities/README.md): Essential household infrastructure supplied as an ongoing service. Examples: electricity supply, gas supply, water, sewerage and refuse collection.
- [Food services (`R:SV:FD`)](Food/README.md): Preparation and serving of food and drink for consumption. Examples: restaurants, catering, canteens and meal preparation.
- [Transport and delivery (`R:SV:TR`)](Transport/README.md): Movement of people or their possessions between places. Examples: public transport, taxis, courier delivery, removals and vehicle hire for travel.
- [Communication (`R:SV:CM`)](Communication/README.md): Connectivity and communication delivery supplied to people. Examples: telephone service, internet access, messaging and postal correspondence.
- [Financial services (`R:SV:FI`)](Financial/README.md): Handling, transfer, custody, lending or management of money and investments for people. Examples: payments, banking, credit provision, investment management and financial advice.
- [Insurance services (`R:SV:IN`)](Insurance/README.md): Provision, administration and servicing of insurance protection. Examples: underwriting, insurance brokerage and claims handling.
- [Legal services (`R:SV:LG`)](Legal/README.md): Advice, representation and assistance concerning rights and legal obligations. Examples: legal advice, representation, mediation and notarization.
- [Public administration (`R:SV:GV`)](PublicAdministration/README.md): Official administrative services provided to people in their capacity as residents or members of the public. Examples: civil registration, licensing, passport processing and public records access.
- [Social care and support (`R:SV:SC`)](SocialCare/README.md): Practical and social assistance supporting daily life, inclusion and wellbeing. Examples: childcare, elder care, disability support and social assistance casework.
- [Personal care (`R:SV:PC`)](PersonalCare/README.md): Non-clinical care of personal appearance, hygiene and everyday wellbeing. Examples: hairdressing, barbering, beauty treatments and non-clinical grooming.
- [Household services (`R:SV:HM`)](Household/README.md): Routine work performed to operate and care for a household. Examples: cleaning, laundry, gardening and household organization.
- [Maintenance and repair (`R:SV:RP`)](MaintenanceRepair/README.md): Work that preserves, restores or improves the usability of buildings and personal goods. Examples: plumbing, electrical repairs, appliance servicing, vehicle repairs and home renovation.
- [Recreation and culture (`R:SV:RC`)](RecreationCulture/README.md): Access to or participation in leisure, sport, entertainment and cultural experiences. Examples: performances, museums, sports facilities, fitness classes and guided leisure activities.
- [Hospitality and travel (`R:SV:HT`)](HospitalityTravel/README.md): Temporary accommodation and arrangements supporting travel or visits. Examples: hotels, guest accommodation, travel booking and trip planning.
- [Security and emergency assistance (`R:SV:SE`)](SecurityEmergency/README.md): Protection of people and property and immediate assistance in dangerous situations. Examples: alarm monitoring, guarding, fire response, rescue and roadside assistance.
- [Funeral and bereavement services (`R:SV:FU`)](Funeral/README.md): Practical arrangements associated with death, remembrance and disposition of remains. Examples: funeral arrangements, burial, cremation and memorial services.
- [Professional and administrative assistance (`R:SV:PS`)](Professional/README.md): Specialist or administrative help with personal affairs not covered by a more specific service category. Examples: translation, personal tax preparation, document assistance and career advice.
- [Retail and access services (`R:SV:RT`)](Retail/README.md): Services that help people obtain, exchange or temporarily use consumer goods. Examples: retail assistance, marketplace intermediation and equipment rental.
- [Animal care (`R:SV:AC`)](AnimalCare/README.md): Care of companion animals supplied to the people responsible for them. Examples: veterinary care, pet grooming, boarding and animal training.

Each category is currently a leaf with no defined answer fields. The examples
illustrate scope and do not introduce additional canonical codes, units or data
collection requirements. Classification does not imply consent or entitlement.
