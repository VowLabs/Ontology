# Professional and administrative assistance

Canonical code: `R:SV:PS`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Specialist or administrative help with personal affairs not covered by a more specific service category. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include translation, personal tax preparation, document assistance and career advice.

Use Legal, Financial or another specific category when it describes the primary purpose. An occupation or named professional is not a child service definition.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
