# Financial services

Canonical code: `R:SV:FI`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Handling, transfer, custody, lending or management of money and investments for people. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include payments, banking, credit provision, investment management and financial advice.

Finance remains the canonical home for financial facts and products. Insurance coverage services have their own category below.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
