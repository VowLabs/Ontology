# Public administration

Canonical code: `R:SV:GV`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Society / Services](../README.md).

Official administrative services provided to people in their capacity as residents or members of the public. Use this classification for a service offering or activity whose
intended customers are individuals or households, regardless of provider or
funding model. Offerings aimed at businesses belong to Business / Services
(`B:SV`).
Examples include civil registration, licensing, passport processing and public records access.

This classifies administrative purpose, not public funding. Publicly funded healthcare and education belong in their respective categories.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples are illustrative, not additional canonical codes. This is a service
classification, with no answer type, units or implied permission to disclose data.
