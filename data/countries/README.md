# Countries reference data

Versioned dataset: `countries`. Its entries are data, not ontology definitions or
canonical tags. The service offers listing, lookup, search and pagination at
`/v1/datasets/countries/records`. The corresponding definition is `S:G:CO`.

Country IDs retain their former `G:CO:XX` spelling to preserve stored answers.
Source attribution, pinned revisions and checksums remain in `_support/sources`.
Names and ISO codes are checked against those snapshots on every load.
