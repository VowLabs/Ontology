# Architect

Canonical code: `B:PRO:ARC`. [Definition](index.json).

Designs buildings and their spatial organization, for example a residential architect. Does not mean a software architect or every building designer.

Applies to the work of a person, including a person represented by a contact
record. Use `B:PRO:ARC` as a canonical record tag when this profession is
relevant. It describes the person represented by the contact, not the contact
list owner or the industry of an organization.

This is a classification concept, with no answer fields, value type, units or
choices. It does not attest a qualification, licence, employment relationship
or current practice. No finer subclasses are currently defined.

Parent: [Professions](../README.md).
