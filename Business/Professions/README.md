# Profession

Canonical code: `B:PRO`. [Definition](index.json).

A profession is an occupation or skilled trade practiced by a person. It describes
the kind of work, separate from employer-specific employment (`B:EMP`), a job title,
or evidence of qualification. A person may practice more than one profession;
an organization employing such people does not itself acquire their profession.

Named professions such as Lawyer and Doctor are entries in the
[professions dataset](../../data/professions/README.md), not ontology children.
Use a dataset ID when recording one. No finer subclasses are defined here.
