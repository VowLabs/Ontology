# Business financial services

Canonical code: `B:SV:FI`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Business / Services](../README.md).

Financial service offerings aimed at businesses. The offering's intended customer determines this classification;
payment alone does not. Examples include business banking, merchant payment processing, business credit and treasury services.

Financial products and facts retain their canonical definitions under Finance. Personal banking belongs to R:SV:FI.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples introduce no additional codes, units, answer types or permissions.
