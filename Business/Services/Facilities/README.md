# Facilities operations

Canonical code: `B:SV:FM`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Business / Services](../README.md).

Operating and maintaining premises used by an organization. Apply this classification to offerings aimed at business and organizational
customers, including companies, public bodies and nonprofits.
Examples include workplace cleaning, facilities management and commercial building maintenance.

Household care belongs to R:SV:HM and personal property repair to R:SV:RP. The building itself remains an asset.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
Examples are illustrative and do not create further canonical codes. There are
no answer types, units or implied verification requirements at this node.
