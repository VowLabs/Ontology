# Human resources

Canonical code: `B:SV:HR`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Business / Services](../README.md).

Helping an organization recruit, administer and support its workforce. Apply this classification to offerings aimed at business and organizational
customers, including companies, public bodies and nonprofits.
Examples include recruitment, staffing, benefits administration and outsourced HR operations.

An employment record belongs to B:EMP; individual career guidance belongs to R:SV:PS. Workforce training offered to employers belongs to B:SV:ED.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
Examples are illustrative and do not create further canonical codes. There are
no answer types, units or implied verification requirements at this node.
