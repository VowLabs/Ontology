# Procurement and supply-chain support

Canonical code: `B:SV:SC`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Business / Services](../README.md).

Helping an organization source, store and distribute operational inputs and outputs. Apply this classification to offerings aimed at business and organizational
customers, including companies, public bodies and nonprofits.
Examples include procurement outsourcing, warehousing and distribution coordination.

Movement of a person or their possessions for personal use belongs to R:SV:TR. Orders, fulfilment and settlement are separate Commerce or Finance records.
