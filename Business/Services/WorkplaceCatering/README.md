# Workplace catering

Canonical code: `B:SV:FD`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Business / Services](../README.md).

Food-service offerings aimed at business and organizational customers. The offering's intended customer determines this classification;
payment alone does not. Examples include staff canteen operation, office catering contracts and corporate event catering.

Individual and household food-service offerings belong to R:SV:FD. Food ingredients and dietary facts remain separate concepts.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples introduce no additional codes, units, answer types or permissions.
