# Office and administrative support

Canonical code: `B:SV:AD`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Business / Services](../README.md).

Performing routine administrative operations on behalf of an organization. Apply this classification to offerings aimed at business and organizational
customers, including companies, public bodies and nonprofits.
Examples include document processing, reception, scheduling and outsourced customer support.

Personal document assistance belongs to R:SV:PS. Customer relationships and support requests are separate records under Commerce.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).

no answer types, units or implied verification requirements at this node.
