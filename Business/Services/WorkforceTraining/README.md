# Workforce training

Canonical code: `B:SV:ED`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Business / Services](../README.md).

Education and training offerings marketed or supplied to businesses for their workforce. The offering's intended customer determines this classification;
payment alone does not. Examples include staff training, organizational learning programmes and employer-commissioned skills courses.

Individual educational offerings belong to R:SV:ED. The subject taught retains its own knowledge-domain classification.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples introduce no additional codes, units, answer types or permissions.
