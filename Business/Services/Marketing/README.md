# Marketing and sales support

Canonical code: `B:SV:MK`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Business / Services](../README.md).

Helping an organization research, reach and serve its market. Apply this classification to offerings aimed at business and organizational
customers, including companies, public bodies and nonprofits.
Examples include advertising, market research, campaign management and outsourced sales operations.

Personal entertainment and cultural participation belong to R:SV:RC. A sale, offer or order is a Commerce record rather than this service type.
