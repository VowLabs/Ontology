# Workplace health services

Canonical code: `B:SV:HC`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Business / Services](../README.md).

Health-service programmes offered to businesses for their workforce. The offering's intended customer determines this classification;
payment alone does not. Examples include occupational health contracts, employer health screening and workplace clinical services.

Individual care offerings belong to R:SV:HC. Personal medical facts remain under Health and classification does not grant employer access to them.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples introduce no additional codes, units, answer types or permissions.
