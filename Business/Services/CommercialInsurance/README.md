# Commercial insurance services

Canonical code: `B:SV:IN`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Business / Services](../README.md).

Insurance offerings aimed at business and organizational customers. The offering's intended customer determines this classification;
payment alone does not. Examples include commercial coverage, business insurance brokerage and corporate claims administration.

Insurance policy records remain B:IN and products remain in Finance. Consumer insurance services belong to R:SV:IN.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
The examples introduce no additional codes, units, answer types or permissions.
