# Technical and quality assurance

Canonical code: `B:SV:TC`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).
Parent: [Business / Services](../README.md).

Specialist work that evaluates or supports the quality and suitability of an organization’s outputs or processes. Apply this classification to offerings aimed at business and organizational
customers, including companies, public bodies and nonprofits.
Examples include product testing, engineering assessment, inspection and certification support.

Scientific knowledge and methods remain under Science. Healthcare offered to individuals belongs to R:SV:HC; workplace health contracts offered to employers belong to B:SV:HC; certification is not implied merely by this classification.

No finer classifications or answer fields are currently defined (`Children: {}`).
Examples are illustrative and do not create further canonical codes. There are
no answer types, units or implied verification requirements at this node.
