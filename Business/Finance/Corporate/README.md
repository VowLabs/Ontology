# Corporate

Canonical code: `B:F:CO`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).

Financial affairs of companies and other operating organizations.

Parent: [B:F](../README.md).

Applies to organizational treasury, capital budgeting and business financing. Personal finance concerns households; Public concerns public-sector finances. The legal identity of an organization remains under Identity.

This is an organizing classification, not an answer field or a claim about a subject.
It defines no answer type, units, choices or value constraints. Classification
does not establish ownership, affiliation, eligibility or verified truth.

No finer subclasses or answer fields are currently defined.
