# Personal

Canonical code: `B:F:PE`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).

Financial affairs of individuals and households.

Parent: [B:F](../README.md).

Applies to household budgeting, personal savings, borrowing and retirement planning. Shared account, asset, loan and product definitions remain directly under Finance; this category does not duplicate them. Business includes individuals’ economic concerns, not only companies.

This is an organizing classification, not an answer field or a claim about a subject.
It defines no answer type, units, choices or value constraints. Classification
does not establish ownership, affiliation, eligibility or verified truth.
