# Home

Canonical code: `HOME`. Definition: [index.json](index.json).

Home organizes domestic life, living spaces and the care and organization of a
household. It applies to household contexts such as housekeeping, furnishing a
living space and home maintenance, whether a dwelling is owned or rented.

Parent: [VowLabs ontology](../README.md).

This is an organizing classification, not an answer field. No finer subclasses
or answer fields are currently defined; it has no answer type, units, choices
or value constraints. Classification does not establish residence or ownership.

Contact addresses remain in Identity (`I:C:AD`); property asset records remain
in Business:Finance (`B:F:A:P`), and household financial affairs belong to
Personal finance (`B:F:PE`). Food preferences, dietary restrictions and health
information belong to Food & Health (`H`). Home does not duplicate those records.
