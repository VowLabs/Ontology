# Organization types

Canonical code: `I:O:K`. These are choices for Organization type (`I:O:T`),
not user records or repeatable collections. All have `Collection: false` and
`Composite: false`. Each choice is terminal; no finer subclasses exist.
The organization declares its form according to its formation jurisdiction.
The vocabulary does not verify incorporation or tax status.

- **Sole Proprietorship** — `I:O:K:SP`: the declared Sole Proprietorship form.
- **Limited Liability Company (LLC)** — `I:O:K:LLC`: the declared Limited Liability Company (LLC) form.
- **General Partnership** — `I:O:K:GP`: the declared General Partnership form.
- **Limited Partnership (LP)** — `I:O:K:LP`: the declared Limited Partnership (LP) form.
- **Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)** — `I:O:K:LLP`: the declared Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) form.
- **S Corporation** — `I:O:K:SC`: the declared S Corporation form.
- **C Corporation** — `I:O:K:CC`: the declared C Corporation form.
- **Privately Held Corporation** — `I:O:K:PRIVATE`: the declared Privately Held Corporation form.
- **Publicly Traded Corporation** — `I:O:K:PUBLIC`: the declared Publicly Traded Corporation form.
- **Nonprofit Organization** — `I:O:K:NP`: the declared Nonprofit Organization form.
- **Cooperative** — `I:O:K:COOP`: the declared Cooperative form.
- **Trust** — `I:O:K:TR`: the declared Trust form.
- **Government Entity** — `I:O:K:GOV`: the declared Government Entity form.
- **Unincorporated Association** — `I:O:K:UA`: the declared Unincorporated Association form.
- **Foundation** — `I:O:K:FD`: the declared Foundation form.
- **Corporation** — `I:O:K:CORP`: the declared Corporation form.
